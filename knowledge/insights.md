# Insights

Lessons deeper than one Blender feature, with the evidence. The rule each produced lives in the file after the arrow; change the rule there, not here. Add an insight when a session changes how we should approach the next project.

- **Find out how the reference was really made.** In `wonder-printed-plastic` the first idea (a physical diffuser blurs the print) was wrong: the references were blurred digitally, then laser-printed. Building the real process raised the score more than any tuning. In `cyber-deck-v2` finding the original artwork (Fusion 360 + KeyShot, a 4K copy, a CAD line render) turned "flat plates" into "filleted CAD solids" as the v01 mechanism. → `research-reference`
- **Build the research's finding first.** In `printed-plastic` the research said "blur in 2D before the print" before v01. The build modelled physical depth instead and plateaued at 6.1–6.8 until v13 used the research, about 70 minutes lost. → `research-reference` step 6
- **Most early "look" problems are render bugs.** Out-of-focus DOF, aliasing and a flipped angle were each reviewed as texture for several rounds. → `review-render`, correctness pass
- **A repeated complaint means the mechanism is wrong.** → `review-render` Rules
- **Reviewers are noisy instruments.** One score is ±0.4. Trust trends and recurring complaints, and compare bests of three, not single rounds. → `review-render` Stopping
- **Measure before you ask for an opinion.** Pixel metrics are cheap; reviews are expensive and noisy. → `tools/metrics.py`, `review-render` step 2
- **What is not measured does not improve.** Until the scoreboard, no retro could say whether a process change made experiments faster or cheaper. → `capture-learnings` step 1
- **Build every value into the script.** A scene that rebuilds from `build.py` can branch, compare and roll back; a hand-edited `.blend` cannot. → `CLAUDE.md` conventions
- **Expose the controls a designer will reach for, one control in one place.** `wonder-popart` (one node per material, plain-language inputs with ranges) was the hand-off that worked. `printed-plastic` spread one blur radius over three nodes and a custom property. → `finish-experiment` step 1
- **Show progress without being asked.** The user asked "are you finished?" 43 minutes into a long loop with no view of progress. → `CLAUDE.md` conventions, `PROGRESS.md`
- **For a mirror-like material, the environment is the material.** In `wonder-minidisc` the diffraction shader was right from v01; the look only arrived once the studio was designed from the disc's mirror direction (a black card there, small lights at chosen angles, big lights hidden from reflections). Design the reflected world before tuning the shader. → proposed mirror-direction helper in `process/improvements.md`. It holds for dark satin plastic too: in `cyber-model` every flat top mirrored the softbox (albedo 0 still rendered 120–137 sRGB), and no albedo change could fix it until the light moved off the mirror direction. Render the object with base colour black before tuning its tone.
- **Isolate before you guess.** About 30 test renders went into "why is the disc white", one guessed cause at a time. One render per suspect (mirror only, lobes only, lights off, subject alone, clear vs tinted) found each cause at once. In `wax-seal` three rounds of "plastic, purple outlines, no groove" came from one cause, which a single scatter-0 render found. → `review-render`, correctness pass
- **A forced change of approach broke a plateau that tuning could not.** `wax-seal` stalled at 6.2–6.6 on a heightfield. A fork with a seed-drawn approach (SDF sculpt, sun, side-by-side reviews) won blind calibration 6.6 over 5.4 in 6 rounds. Its first draw (fluid sim) was infeasible, and rerolling from the same seed kept the spirit without sinking more hours. → consider a seed-drawn fork when a loop stops on the slope rule (Proposed in `process/improvements.md`)
- **Build at the target resolution of the style; do not make detail and remove it.** `roman-model` spent three rounds on a subdivided body decimated to low poly, which came out as smooth tubes with bands. Placing the planes directly (a few controlled rings, jitter, triangulate) got the look in one round. The same holds wherever a style is defined by its coarseness: pixel art, low-poly, papercraft. → `knowledge/gotchas/modelling.md`
- **After any step that can fail silently, assert something countable.** In `cyber-model` an Exact boolean returned an empty mesh or dropped cuts with no message, and the wrapper script hid the one line that said why. Each time it looked like a design problem (a silver slab, missing vents, a "crash") and cost a round or an hour, about 1.5 hours in all. A polygon count, the z-levels of the result, or a result line printed after every risky call would have caught each one. → `knowledge/gotchas/geometry.md`, `tools/blender.sh`
- **Rules in prose decay; rules in tools and hooks hold.** Every artifact check held across seven loops, while the prose steps beside them (pixel gate, fewer crops, scatter-0, no zsh loops) were each skipped within two experiments. A step worth keeping is worth a tool or a hook; a longer sentence is not a fix. → `capture-learnings` step 6 (word budget), `tools/review_round.py`, `tools/preflight.py`
- **Ten rounds of tuning top out near 7.** Every loop since the pipeline ended between 6.4 and 7.3 against 8.5, and every plateau broke on a new mechanism, never on values. → `review-render` Stopping (fork on plateau)
- **A global tell hides behind local fixes.** In `aztechno-building` every round fixed its top-ranked content problem (relief, weathering, glass, outer thirds) and the score sat at 5.3–5.9: the image formation (a soft filtered pinhole beside a sharpened JPEG) made every region read rendered at once, and no reviewer named it. A fork advisor found it by measuring fine-detail energy per region. When scores stall on varied complaints, measure what is constant across all regions. → `research-reference` step 5 (`tools/sharpness.py`)
