# Process improvements

How the system changes itself. `/capture-learnings` adds to this at the end of each session. Newest first.

Every Changed entry ends with a **Check**: what the next experiment should show if the change worked. The retro marks each check `held`, `failed` or `unobserved`, with the date. Two `failed` marks → rewrite or revert the change. Two `held` marks and no `failed` → the entry is settled and moves to [improvements-archive.md](improvements-archive.md). Entries from before 2026-09-27 got their checks retroactively.

## Proposed
- **`sweep.sh` tile for wide or multi-subject frames.** Its tile of six 3200×1128 sheets came out 7720×451 and unreadable; `plotter-blend` cropped a grid by hand each time (8 sweeps). Give `sweep.sh` a `--crop x0,y0,x1,y1` (fractions) and a column count.
- **Crops for a contact sheet.** `review_round.py` cuts fixed 512 px crops at given points; a catalogue sheet of 770 px cells needed a scratch helper to print each cell's centre (`plotter-forms`, every round). Give `review_round.py` / `crops.py` a `--grid cols,rows[,size]` mode that crops named cells.
- **Several reviewer briefs in one experiment.** A second series with another instrument had to move to a sibling folder (`plotter-forms`), because `review_round.py` reads one `REVIEWER_PROMPT.md` and one Budget. A `--series <name>` (own prompt, budget and version prefix) would keep a follow-up in its experiment. Second case: `apartment-model` round two (materials) swapped `REVIEWER_PROMPT.md` in place; the lab-watch stall guard then compared v13–v15 against the shell brief's v10–v12 every round (slope and repeat rules tripped on a new instrument), the budget had to be summed, and the calibration would have overwritten the first (fixed: see Changed). Third case: round three (sofa) — the stall guard and `review_round.py`'s round-1 checks now read the round from PROGRESS.md's "## Round … (from vNN)" (see Changed); the summed budget and the shared `REVIEWER_PROMPT.md` swap remain.
- **Self-intersection curves as strokes** in `plot_svg.py`: lines on a self-crossing surface end in mid-air where another sheet hides them (`plotter-forms` open item).

(None open. All eleven were built on 2026-10-01; see Changed.)
