## 4. Problems, ranked

1. **Seat tops too bright and flat** (row 1 left, row 3). They are 10–20 % over the paint ratio and read as slabs with a fat rolled front. The photos show a gentle dome falling to a crisper edge. Fix: seat-top albedo or sheen down about 15 %, a 2–3 cm dome, a 3 cm front roll with the flange on the corner.
2. **Flanges read as piping** (row 1 left arm, row 2 near arm). The render has a thin, even, pale line. The photo lip is about 1 cm wide, wavy, higher in contrast, with a dark underside. The ears are needle spikes (row 1 arm top front, ottoman back-left corner), not soft pinched folds. Fix: widen the flange, add waviness and a shadowed underside, make the ears 1.5 cm bent folds.
3. **No wear** (rows 2–3). The photos show horizontal ripples on the back fronts and slack seats. The render is pristine, apart from an odd dashed crease along the row 2 back top. Fix: 3–5 shallow ripples on each back front, a slight seat dish; remove the crease.

## 5. Research check

It agrees on modules, proud seats, back blocks, and dark feet with a shadow gap. It contradicts the flange finding: this reads as piping. The warm greige is missing: the fabric matches the wall's hue, where in photo 1 it is about 10 % warmer than the paint.

## 6. What 8.5 needs

- Seat tops 15 % darker, domed, with a tighter front edge.
- A wide, wavy, shadowed flange with soft ears.
- Ripples on the backs, slack on the seats.
- Fabric 10 % warmer than the wall, with coarser slub contrast at 1:1.
