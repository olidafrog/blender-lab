## 4. Problems, ranked

1. **Arms and back blocks too round (rows 1 and 2).** The row 1 room-end arm reads as a bolster. The back blocks read as loaves with V-gaps. The photos show square blocks with about 2–3 cm edge radius, flat tops and back blocks on a straight seam. **Fix:** edge radius about 2.5 cm; puff as a 1–1.5 cm crown in the face centre; close the back-block gaps.
2. **Seats and backs too bright; hue matches the wall (rows 1 and 3).** In the photo the fabric is warmer than the wall (B/R 0.91 vs 0.99). In the render both are 0.84, so the sofa melts into the beige walls. **Fix:** cut seat and back value about 20 % (albedo or occlusion); warm the fabric against the paint.
3. **Taut surfaces, mechanical flange (row 3 crops, row 2 near arm).** Photo seats dip and crease where people sit; the render's are drum-tight. The flange is uniform piping with no wavy lip and no visible ears. **Fix:** 1–2 cm sat-in dip with low-frequency wrinkles; wavy flange profile; pinched ears.

## 5. Research check

Sizes, heights and the 14 cm step agree. The render contradicts "thin wavy lip … pinched ears": its flange is straight piping. It reads "softly puffed faces" as round edges. The fabric is cooler against the wall than the swatch (183, 176, 166) implies.

## 6. What 8.5 needs

- Square the arms and back blocks: small edge radius, shallow face crown.
- Seats and backs about 20 % darker; fabric warmer than the wall.
- Sat-in dips and creases; wavy flange and ears.
- Weaker streaks in the weave.
