# Calibration review: sofa, A and B

Luma: my boxes on the sheet, scaled by the photo's own ratio.

## A

1. **Score: 6.3 / 10** (Shape 6.5, Placement 8.0, Detail 6.0, Fabric 5.3)
2. **Targets**
   - Photo 1: seat tops ~1.14 vs 1.24, ottoman top ~1.07 vs 1.28, ottoman front ~0.59 vs 0.74, back front ~0.77 vs 0.89. All missed (low).
   - Photo 3: seat ~1.03 vs 1.16, arm inner ~1.10 vs 1.39. Missed.
   - Edges: within 10–15 px in rows 1–2. Hit.
3. **What works**
   - Module sizes, seats proud of the arms; red edges sit on the photo edges.
   - Dark round feet and a shadow gap (row 1, ottoman).
4. **Problems, ranked**
   1. **Fabric too dark and too warm** (all rows). Seat RGB 178,168,155 vs photo 200,194,183. The paint is yellow too (172,157,138 vs 172,167,154): a frame-wide warm cast plus ~15 % low albedo. Fix: neutral white balance, then +15 % base colour.
   2. **Hard boxes** (row 3 seat front; row 2 arm and seat front). Photo: a 6–8 cm roll and bulging faces. Render: about 2 cm and flat. Fix: a bigger edge roll and 1–2 cm of face inflation.
   3. **Flanges thin, straight and faint** (row 2 arm front; row 1 ottoman). The photo lip is about 1 cm and wavy, with ears. Fix: widen it, add waviness and pinch the corners. Also, the ottoman's front-top edge sags (row 1); the photo's is straight.
5. **Research check:** It agrees on construction, feet and size. It contradicts "puffed faces" and the greige swatch (A is browner).
6. **What 8.5 needs:** neutral WB and +15 % value; rolled edges and puffed faces; wavy flanges with ears; higher-contrast slub (row 2 photo).

## B

1. **Score: 6.5 / 10** (Shape 6.7, Placement 8.0, Detail 6.3, Fabric 5.8)
2. **Targets:** Luma within 0.01 of A: all missed. Hue hits: paint 166,159,147 and seat 177,170,162 have the photo's balance. Edges hit.
3. **What works:** The hue matches rows 1 and 3. The back blocks have soft ripples like the photo (row 2, left). The ottoman edge is straight.
4. **Top problem:** The same hard boxes as A (row 3 seat roll; row 2 flat seat front). Then the fabric is about 15 % dark, worst on the ottoman top. Same fixes, without the WB change.
5. **Research check:** It agrees, except for the puffed faces.
6. **What 8.5 needs:** +15 % fabric value; rolled edges and puffed faces; wavy flanges with ears; stronger slub.

## Which is closer

B is closer. Its white balance matches the photos' fabric and paint, and its back ripples follow the references. A has the same shape under a yellow-brown cast.
