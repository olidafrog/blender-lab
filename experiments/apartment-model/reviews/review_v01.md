# Review v01 — apartment-model

Rows are photos 1, 2, 3, 5, 6, 7, 8.

1. **Score:** 6.8 / 10. Layout 7.5, Windows 6.0, Structure 6.8, Built-ins 6.0.

2. **Targets**
- Window wall (photo 2): jambs 3–5 px, sills 3 px, beam end <3 px, arch tops 6 px low: hit. Centre pier cap 8–10 px low: missed (20 px in photo 1).
- Mezzanine front (photos 3, 6): girder, soffit, column within 5 px: hit. Internal windows: bottom 12 px low (photo 3), 20–25 px low with the left jamb 30 px right (photo 6): missed.
- Ceiling lines and corners: hit in every photo except 5, where the top-left corner and beam edge are 17–20 px low: missed.
- Kitchen: counters and island within 8 px in photos 7 and 8: hit. The right run's far end is 25 px short in photo 8: missed.

3. **What works**
- Corners, downstands and ceiling lines land in almost every row.
- The girder, central beam and splayed pilaster heads fit within 3 px in photo 3.
- The split under the mezzanine is right: dining and bookcase, hall door, column, kitchen L and island.

4. **Problems, ranked**
1. **Internal steel windows.** The openings are about 12 cm too tall at the sill. In photo 6 the frame sits about 30 px behind the wall face. Fix: raise the sill; set the frame nearly flush with the room face.
2. **Arched windows.** Each window has one casement. The photos show two, hinged at the outer jambs. In the oblique views (photos 1, 5) the outline sits 10–15 px inside each jamb, and the apex is 17–20 px low. Fix: add the left leaf, check the frame setback in the reveal, and raise the apex to about 3.82 m.
3. **Built-ins and details.** The open shelves are about 40 % short (photo 7: 450–555 px against 395–570 px). In photo 8 the column sits about 10 cm behind the fascia; the photos show it flush. No radiators are visible. Coplanar faces on the fascia and kitchen walls speckle the overlays. Fix: run the shelves wall to wall, extend the kitchen run, bring the column forward, add the radiators, delete the duplicates.

5. **Research check:** Width, ceiling, girder, soffit, sill and beam values agree; the arches read as segmental. One contradiction: at 78 px/m in photo 2, the pier caps sit at about 1.6 m, not 1.5 m.

6. **What 8.5 needs**
- Fix the internal window sill and setback.
- Add the second casement; fix the oblique fit.
- Pier caps at 1.6 m; column flush with the fascia.
- Full-length shelves and kitchen run; radiators.
- Remove coplanar faces.
