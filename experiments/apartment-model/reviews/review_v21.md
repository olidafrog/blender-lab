## 3. What works

## 4. Problems, ranked

1. **Flat form lighting (rows 2 and 3).** Seat front/top is 0.64 vs 0.42 in photo 2, and 0.91 vs 0.64 in photo 3. The arm front is 0.61 vs 0.33. Fronts are too bright and go tan-orange (R−B 33 vs 16). **Fix:** strengthen the window/sky key on horizontal faces. Cut the warm fill until row 2's front/top is about 0.45.
2. **Boxes, not cushions (row 1 ottoman; row 2 arm and backs).** The photo ottoman has a domed top and sides that bulge out from the base. The render has a flat top and planar sides. Arms and backs are flat slabs. **Fix:** dome the tops 2–3 cm. Puff the faces 1–1.5 cm. Add shallow pull-wrinkles to the backs. Remove the seat crease.
3. **Missing construction detail.** The flanges are thinner and fainter than the photo's pale lips. There are no corner ears and no visible shadow gap under the blocks. Mid-scale mottle makes the ottoman read as speckled stone (row 1 panel). **Fix:** use raised 1 cm flanges with ears. Set the feet back so a dark gap shows. Halve the low-frequency slub contrast.

## 5. Research check

The image contradicts "domed tops and puffed faces", "pinched ears" and "a dark shadow gap under every block". Sizes and seats proud of the arms agree.

## 6. What 8.5 needs

- A top-down key and less warm fill.
- Domed, puffed blocks.
- Lit flange lips and ears.
- A shadow gap.
- Less mid-scale mottle.
