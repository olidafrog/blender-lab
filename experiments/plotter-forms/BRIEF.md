## References

`ref_sheet.png` (copied from `plotter-blend`): the user gave no images for this series; it sets the line character (thin monoline, technical-illustration feel). The reviewer judges against the brief and the written criteria in `reviews/REVIEWER_PROMPT.md`.

## Target

Score 8.5 from the reviewer, for the catalogue as a whole.

## Budget

5 review rounds. no fork: this is a catalogue of about forty forms, so there is no single mechanism to replace; a weak form is fixed or dropped.

## Deliverables

- `output/svg/<form>.svg` — one plot per form: strokes only, in mm, hidden lines removed where the form is opaque.
- `output/FINAL_plotter-forms.png` — the catalogue sheet (a raster of the SVGs; what the reviewer scores), plus one sheet per family.
- `output/plotter-forms.blend` — every form as its own "Plot …" collection with sliders, a `HOW_TO_TWEAK` text block and the "Export SVG" script.
