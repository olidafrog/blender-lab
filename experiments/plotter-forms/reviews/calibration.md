# Calibration review: A and B

42 of 50 cells are identical.

## A

1. **Score:** 6.7 / 10. 3D read 6.6, hidden lines 6.0, plot readiness 6.5, variety 8.0.
2. **Targets:** 50 forms in 5 families: hit. Lines through a surface: none found, hit. Fragments: none under 1 mm, but visible dashes at the silhouettes of ridgeline-ripple, mobius, dini and hopf-onion: missed. Blots: densest 15 px window 78 % ink (hopf-tumble), lines stay apart at 3×: hit. Lines leaving the cell: 0 px on any edge, hit.
3. **What works:** seashell, klein-ghost, blobs, gravity-wells and flamm are finished plots. The lumpy harmonic bodies and the perspective wave-field read as volumes.
4. **Problems, ranked**
   1. **ridgeline-pulsar, centre:** lines end in mid-air with nothing in front, so it reads as torn scraps. Fix: skyline occlusion (each row against the running maximum of the rows in front), not a depth tolerance.
   2. **Spheres (harmonic-fine, sphere-noise), limb:** contours stop 3–5 mm short of the outline, so each sphere reads as a printed disc. Fix: analytic visibility (p·view > 0) instead of a grazing-angle cut.
   3. **ridgeline-ripple, mobius, dini:** 2–6 mm dashes where the surface goes edge-on. Fix: drop visible runs under 2 mm, and stroke the fold silhouette.
   4. **hopf-tumble, centre:** a see-through tangle with no depth cue. Fix: fewer fibres, or a second pen for the back half.
   5. **knot-slices, catalan:** read flat. Fix: tilt the knot camera about 35°, and sample catalan more finely near its cusps.

## B

1. **Score:** 6.5 / 10. 3D read 6.4, hidden lines 6.2, plot readiness 5.8, variety 7.6.
2. **Targets:** as A, but with 25 isolated strokes of 1–2.4 mm (A has 2): fragments missed.
3. **What works:** ridgeline-pulsar is continuous with tall peaks. The fold outline on ridgeline-ripple makes the crater read.
4. **Top problem:** hopf-links, hopf-tumble and hopf-meridian get a gap at every crossing. They are see-through, so the circles break into 1–3 mm dashes. Also: V-notches on monkey-slices, a doubled gyroid-ball outline, flat harmonic discs.
