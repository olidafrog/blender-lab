- Deep teal/navy: left edge #2e5756, cut-out rim #005f65. Gear #545354 and bars #51504f are neutral grey. **Partial hit.**

- Hot pink #fe6f6b: #d94d50–#dd494b. Darker and redder than v06. **Missed.**

- Micro-type: top band 8–10 px cap height (0.5%). Lockups are fainter. **Missed (7th round).**

3. **What works**
- The left third is teal now, not olive. The palette moves towards ref 02.
- Depth blur works. The small gear is crisp and the big gear melts.
- The cut-out bars with teal and orange rims look photographic.

4. **Problems, ranked**
1. **Centre column: grey veil, no glow (x 600–1000).** Row y=1000 goes teal → #63625f → #6f6d6c → orange. The milk reads as smoked glass, not lit resin. The cream zone sits exactly where the dark parts are, so it can never show. Change the mechanism. Give the plate's scatter a warm cream colour (#f0dcc0). Raise its density until parts under the plate lift to #3a5358–#4f6a6a. Move the cream gel stop to a region with no parts behind it, or pull the parts left by 10–15%. Target: a cream patch of #d8bc98–#e7c69d at least 150 px wide.

2. **Strip at x≈1075 (4th round, unchanged).** A full-height glowing bar that clips to white. Its edges are soft and it sits over the bars, so it may be an emitter seen through the plate, not a reflection. Diagnose first: render once with the strip light's camera and transmission visibility off. Then keep only short, broken glints: pools at the rivets and one piece on the top edge, peak 0.85–0.92.

3. **Type does not read as moulded.** The top band breaks into dots at 1:1. "Wonder" is flat grey fill, and only the "l" has a glint. The vertical line on the pink is fragments. Seven rounds of size tweaks have failed, so set it in geometry. Use cap height 22–28 px, a medium-weight mono face, 0.3–0.5 mm extrude and a 0.05 mm bevel. Rake the relief with a low top light.

5. **Research check**
- Blur grows with depth: agrees.
- Opalescence (blue skin, warm core): contradicted. No cool sheen anywhere.
- Raised type read by highlights and shadow: contradicted. It reads as print or dots.
- Palette: teal and pink are right. Cream and saturated orange are still missing, so the orange washes to apricot-brown.

6. **What 8.5 needs**
- A cream-lit milk that lifts the parts to teal-grey, with a visible cream band.
- Remove the strip. Add wet pools at the rivets.
- A large, dim, cool softbox in the coat over the upper-left (coat roughness 0.3–0.4) to reach #9fb8b7.
- Moulded type at 22–28 px with a raking light.
- A 0.2–0.5 mm surface warp, faint scratches, 1–2% grain and brighter edges that pipe light. The plate is still perfectly flat and clean. The corner brackets and domed bolts look like toys.
