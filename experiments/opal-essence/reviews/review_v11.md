2. **Targets**
- Background: #000000 at the edges and in the cut-outs. **Hit.**
- Deep teal #012437–#085c62: #025c61 only on the cut-out rim (x 228, y 1324). Parts read neutral grey: gear #383d41, T-bar #4e4d4f, low bar #424041. **Missed.**
- Sage #7c9f93: #82af91. **Hit.**
- Cream #e7c69d: #e9c99c. **Hit.**
- Amber #fdbb55: nearest #f5ab68. **Missed** (salmon).
- Orange #fca321: most saturated #f4a369, S 0.57. **Missed** (peach, fourth round).
- Coral #fe6f6b: #f67171. **Hit.**
- Opal blue #9fb8b7: nearest #a3b4c1 is a screw head, not the plate. **Missed** (fourth round).
- Micro-type cap height: about 1.2% on the top strip. **Hit.**

3. **What works**
- Depth blur still sells the see-through. The small gear's teeth are crisp and the big gear melts.
- The brackets now have chrome edge lines. The screws have domed heads that catch a highlight.
- "OPAL ESSENCE / SPECIMEN" micro-type reads as moulded relief at 1:1.

4. **Problems, ranked**
1. **Left half: the parts are still grey, with red-brown halos** (gear L 0.24, h 207, near-zero saturation; halos #684d4b at x 300 y 700, #725c57 at x 820 y 1330). The teal anodising did not survive the milk. The frost layer lays a neutral white veil over any dark base. This is round three, so change the mechanism. Drive the plate's scatter/volume colour from the same gradient ramp, so the milk over the left third scatters teal (#0a4a50), not white. Find the warm source of the halos with a light-linking solo pass and kill it. Target: parts L 0.10–0.25, hue 180–200, S ≥ 0.35.
2. **Whole plate: still a lit gel, not a wet resin slab.** At 1:1 there is no puddled highlight, no roughness breakup, no warp and no thick lit edge. The only speculars are on the hardware. The v10 coat fix did not land. Check it with a glossy-only AOV. Add Coat weight 1, roughness 0.02–0.05 with noise roughness from 0.02 to 0.2, and a large strip card at the mirror angle. Bevel the plate edge 2–3 mm.
3. **Bottom left: the logotype is a flat grey fill** (#64676c). The stray vertical line still cuts the "d" ("Won|er"). The dark box behind it frames it like a label. Fourth round. Find and delete the object that makes the line. Union the glyphs into the plate face with a 0.3 mm bevel, so it reads by edge glints. The right-edge vertical micro-type is still loose dots; drop it or raise its relief.

5. **Research check**
- Agrees: blur grows with depth. The micro-type relief is subtle.
- Contradicts: no opal response (no blue-white skin, no warm core). The top strip (#7d6256–#9e8578, grey #4e5053 on the left) reads as a smoked overlay, not milky resin.

6. **What 8.5 needs**
- Teal-tinted milk over dark parts, and no warm halos.
- A visible wet coat and a thick lit edge.
- A logotype moulded into the face, with no stray line.
- An orange band at S ≥ 0.8. Push the gel emission, or use AgX Punchy.
- A blue-white Fresnel tint (#9fb8b7) at glancing angles.
