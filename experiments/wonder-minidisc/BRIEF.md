# wonder-minidisc

## Brief

> Create a new experiment where your goal will be to create a material that has the qualities of a compact disc or mini disc record. I'd also be interested in exploring this colored plastic material surrounding it and having good control over the color. I want you to use the wonder logo mark as the object. So the two challenges here are 1. The cd material 2. The plastic casing, both for it to feel materially correct and to model the details of the case and the shape language correct. The case could follow the shape of the logo mark but would need to be a case and have some air gap between.
>
> There's probably quite a lot of tutorials on how to achieve a cd like material, I want quite a broad colour spectrum on it, similar to the last reference. Feel free to also download any images, references, and materials of the internet in order to achieve the goals you need. This might especially be useful for the plastic which will probably need some light scratches, dust etc. setup a nice bright scene for this and use our regular pipeline for experiment production

## References

- `ref1_fan_holographic.jpg` — folding fan with a pearlescent/holographic film. Take the soft pastel spectrum (pink → mint → lilac → grey) that sweeps across a curved surface, and the concentric fine lines. Bright neutral studio backdrop.
- `ref2_minidisc_red.jpg` — Sony red translucent MiniDisc. Take the coloured translucent plastic: saturated where thick, clear-ish where thin, the disc seen through it as dark/orange sectors, moulded ribs and edges, grey metal shutter, frosted label window, printed white type, reflection on the table, bright white background.
- `ref3_psx_memorycards.jpg` — PlayStation memory cards in opaque and translucent colours. Take the colour range (clear, green, teal, yellow, orange, red), the embossed arrow and logo, and how the PCB shows through tinted plastic. This is the "good control over colour" target.
- `ref4_minidisc_clear.jpg` — clear MiniDisc. The main target for the disc material: a broad spectrum (pink, magenta, violet, green, cyan) in radial sectors, with a mirror-grey hub, clear hub ring with text, and the clear case with its screw bosses, ribs, rails and grey shutter.
