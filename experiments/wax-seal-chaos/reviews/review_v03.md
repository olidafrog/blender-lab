3. **What works**
- The scale, framing, field value and hue all match.
- The steep inner wall at the stamp circle is right, with a dark violet occlusion line (56–64, 46–56, 67–74, against 42, 29, 57 in the reference).
- The emblem is lit from the right: highlights sit on the right edges and shadows on the left. The key direction is correct.

4. **Problems, ranked**
1. **Flat lighting ratio across the whole seal.** The left outer slope and the cast shadow have no deep violet. The rim tops blow out on both sides. The fill is too strong and the key too hot on the rim tops. Cut the world/fill light until the shadow-side rim reaches 60–95 luminance, and drop the key about 0.5 EV. Tint the bounce with the wax: a violet bounce card at the lower left, or let SSS carry a saturated radius into the shadows. The cast shadow needs a warm core at 55–70 near the contact. Values alone have not fixed this over two rounds, so change the light rig, not the material.
2. **The relief reads as machined plastic.** The tops are flat plateaus with a crisp chamfer, so they shade exactly like the field. Pressed wax is pillowed. Use a rounded, smooth-step profile over 40–60% of the stroke width, so the tops dome and catch the key at 220–235. Soften the top corners too.
3. **The surface and rim are too clean.** The field is optically perfect. The "flow lines" are single crisp hairlines, like a stray hair and a small loop at the bottom. The rim is a near-circular two-tier torus with a visible ledge. Replace the hairlines with broad low-relief ridges, about 0.05–0.1 mm high and 1–3 mm wide, plus fine chalky micro-bump. Make the rim one rolled bead with a lobed outline: a pooled lump at the lower right and a crease or fold on the right side.

5. **Research check:** The image contradicts "shadows turn deep and saturated violet", both on the seal and in the cast shadow. It contradicts "the outer outline is an irregular blob". The single rolled bead is broken by a double tier. The key direction and the satin finish agree with the research.

6. **What 8.5 needs**
- A new light ratio: violet shadow side at 60–95, rim tops at 190–215, and a warm cast-shadow core.
- A pillowed emblem profile whose tops read brighter than the field.
- Chalky micro-texture and soft flow ridges in place of the hairlines.
- An irregular, lumpy, single-bead rim.
