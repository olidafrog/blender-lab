4. **Problems, ranked**
1. **Wax reads as soft-touch vinyl (whole seal).** There is no chalk grain, no pitting and no crisp glints. The ref has hard spec pings on the rim (lower left, right lobe). The flow lines are raised threads, like hairs on the surface. Fix: add a micro bump (features 0.05–0.2 mm, strength 0.02–0.05). Vary roughness 0.3–0.5, and use 0.2–0.3 on high-curvature rim areas. Make the flow lines *incised* (0.02–0.05 mm deep), branching and fading, on the field only. Delete the stray loop (lower right). Desaturate so B−G is 15–20.
2. **Cast shadow is a flat grey drop shadow.** It has uniform density over ~440 px, no contact core and no warmth. Fix: halve the key's angular size and raise it to 35–45°. Cut the fill to 5–8% of the key and warm it. The target is a graded shadow with a core near 63, 48, 49. The outer rim's shadow side should fall to ~90–100 with violet bleed.
3. **Silhouette is a faceted near-circle with a stepped skirt.** Straight segments show in full.png. A second flat ledge sits outside the bead (crops 100_1000 and 1000_1050). Fix: use a polar outline with 2–3 low-frequency lobes (±4–8%) and one pooled lobe (+10–15% over 50–70°, lower right). Use at least 256 segments. Make the bead one continuous profile into a ~1 mm meniscus.

5. **Research check**
The render contradicts "irregular blob outline" and "deep, saturated violet shadows". It partly contradicts "thin edges do not glow": the rim top and upper emblem edges carry a pale pink sheen brighter than the field.

6. **What 8.5 needs**
- Micro grain, glints and incised flow lines.
- A graded warm cast shadow and a darker violet rim shadow side.
- An irregular pooled silhouette with no facets or step.
- Relief tops at ~228: crown them 0.05–0.1 mm and lower their roughness slightly.
