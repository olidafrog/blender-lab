3. **What works**

- The field colour and the B > R > G balance.
- The steep inner wall at the stamp circle.

4. **Problems, ranked**
1. **The left outer wall is lighter than the shadow beside it (x 90–150, y 500–1000).** In the reference the wax wall is the darkest thing in the frame (30–54), darker than the paper shadow. Here it is 110 against a 68 shadow. The new bead has a stepped foot: a second ledge catches light at x 144 and reads as a moulded poker chip. This is the third round, and value tweaks have not fixed it. Change the mechanism. Remove the ledge and make one continuous wall of 80–100°, curving into the paper with a fillet of 0.3 mm or less. Then kill the light that fills that wall: set a black world on the left or add a flag, and cut the SSS radius to 0.1–0.3 mm. Target 30–70 luminance with B–G ≥ 20.
2. **The emblem is still a keycap (centre).** The tops are flat and match the field. There is a bright 1 px bevel line on the right edges (239) and a crease on the left base (82, 50, 95) that is too saturated. Use a domed or smoothstep profile across 40–60% of the stroke width, so the tops face the key and reach 220–232. Add a 0.3–0.5 mm base fillet.
3. **The surface and outline are too clean.** One long raised thread runs from the emblem to the rim, and a loop sits at x 450, y 950. Both read as a hair or a crack. The field has no chalky grain, and the rim has no wet glints. The outline is a round, faceted coin with no lobe. Use terrace edges 0.05–0.1 mm high, a micro bump at about 0.2 mm, and a lobe at the lower right plus a pinch at the lower middle.

5. **Research check:** The image contradicts "shadows turn deep and saturated violet": the shadowed wall is grey-mauve at 110. It contradicts "an irregular blob" and "a soft rounded contact with the paper": the foot is stepped. It agrees on the key direction and the steep inner wall.

6. **What 8.5 needs**
- One continuous, steep outer wall with no ledge, and no fill on it, so it reaches 30–70 and turns violet.
- Rim tops at 190–220. Lower the key by about 0.4 EV.
- A domed emblem with a base fillet, tops at 220–232.
- Terrace flow edges and grain in place of the threads.
- A lobed outline at 90% of the frame width.
