# Review v10

1. **Score:** 6.4 / 10

2. **Targets**
- Backdrop TL 111/96: **missed** (+15). TR, BL, BR: hit.
- Share 39/36, median 73/75, p5 15/7, p95 89/92: hit.
- <12 % 3.5/7.6 (0.46): **missed**.
- LCD, grain 16.7/15.4: hit.
- By eye: tops 80–88, hit. Slopes 127–160, **missed** (target 90–105).

3. **What works**
- The S-step is now a broad flat band with a crest fillet and a crease. It is the first version that reads as a moulded step.
- A solid LCD shield carries the pods. No backdrop shows through.
- Layout, camera, dial module, LCD, screws and vents match closely.

4. **Problems, ranked**
1. **The slopes read as polished metal ribbons** (S-band x 780–1300, y 560–720; lower fillets x 830–1300, y 780–900). They measure 127–160, with a sharp specular streak. The reference slopes are 90–105, with a soft gradient. Fix: this is reflection, not albedo. Set slope roughness to 0.5–0.6, or drop "Card Slopes" to 3–5 W. Keep the crest highlight 4 px wide or less at 1600 px.
2. **The LCD shield is a separate grey picture frame** (x 400–920, y 250–600). It is 15–20 mm wide, not 8–10 mm. It is lighter than the body, with a wide glossy pillow bevel. The pods are small and sit in a U-bracket above the shield. In the reference, the shield is black body polymer with a short wall and a tight crease. Two large barrels lie along its top-left edge. Fix: trim the shield to the LCD outline + 9 mm, in body material, with a 3–4 mm 55° wall. Scale the pods about 1.4× and lay them on the shield. Delete the bracket.
3. **The gaps are not black, and the finish is too clean.** The crease under the S-step measures 21. The tops show almost no speckle and few scratches. The lower lid (97) is lighter than the tops. Fix: cut gap channels 3–4 mm deep over an albedo-0.01 liner. Add 20–40 straight hairline scratches per plate. Bring the lid to 60–75.

5. **Research check**
- Sloped steps with a crest fillet and a foot crease: agrees.
- Slopes lighter than tops: right direction, but it overshoots into specular.
- Strong fine bead-blast speckle: contradicted. The tops are smooth.
- The backdrop grain is harsh salt-and-pepper at 1:1 (bl crop). The reference grain is finer and softer.

6. **What 8.5 needs**
- Slopes at 90–105, matte, no streak.
- A black 8–10 mm LCD shield with large pods lying on it.
- Creases below 12, <12 % at 5.3 or more, backdrop TL at 108 or less.
- Visible speckle and hairline scratches on the tops.
- Softer, finer backdrop grain.
