4. **Problems, ranked**

The reference has a satin aluminium arm (about 125) on the left flank. It runs from under the pods, wraps the dial and ends at a hex-screw lug beside ON/OFF. The dial sits inboard. The gear has about 36 square teeth, 2 mm deep, black with lit crests. Fix: trace the arm from `ref_front.jpg` (x 1110–1300, y 620–1010), extrude it 2 mm and seat it on the shell. Move the dial about 8 mm inboard. Stop reshaping the teardrop.
2. **The LCD bezel is a chrome tube (up to 255).** A chrome rod runs down the LCD's left side (x 480–520). Last round's cap is still on the top-left corner (x 505–530, y 445–475) and cuts off "CURRENT SOURCE". The reference bezel is a flat satin grey frame, about 3 mm wide (85–130), with corner screws and a dark gasket. Fix: a flat rounded-rectangle ring in the bracket gunmetal. Delete the rod and the cap.
3. **The polymer is smooth, and the slopes do not lift.** The 0.25 mm bump (1.6 px) averages out. The reference shows light specks and more scratches. Fix: albedo and roughness speckle at 0.5–0.8 mm, ±10–15 % value, roughness 0.40–0.50. Roughness values did not lift the slopes. Add a large dim card behind the camera, linked to the device only. Camera-facing slopes reflect it.

5. **Research check** — The fillets, crease and screws agree. The render has no bead-blast speckle, and its slopes are not lighter than its tops. At 1:1 the backdrop grain looks crumpled and cell-like, not a fine speckle.
