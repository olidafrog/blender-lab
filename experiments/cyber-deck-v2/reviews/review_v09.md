# Review v09

1. **Score:** 6.3 / 10

2. **Targets**
- Backdrop TL 111/96: **missed** (+15). TR, BL, BR: hit.
- Share 39/36, median 74/75, p5 15/7, p95 89/92: hit.
- <12 % 3.6/7.6 (0.47): **missed**, worse than before.
- LCD, grain 16.7/15.4: hit.
- By eye: tops 80–88 hit. Slopes **missed**: too narrow to read. Only the crest highlight reaches 90–95.

3. **What works**
- The dial bracket is satin aluminium and the gear has square teeth. It reads as machined metal.
- Layout, framing, camera and the LCD match closely.
- Screws, vents and the INSERT latch are right.

4. **Problems, ranked**
1. **The S-step and the shells read as rolled beads, not moulded steps** (x 780–1300, y 560–900). The upper plate sits about 2 mm above the lower level, on a narrow round lip. The reference drops 6–8 mm over a flat 45–55° face, 20–30 px wide, with a 1 mm crest fillet. The crests shade lumpy: the bead-blast bump is too coarse. Fix: raise the upper plate 4–6 mm. Loft the step as a flat band, not a fillet. Set the polymer bump to 0. Put the speckle in albedo and roughness only (0.3–0.6 mm, ±10 % value).
2. **The LCD shield is a thin rim with a grey rod round it** (x 470–900, y 250–600). The backdrop shows between the pods and the LCD. The reference has a solid black shield, 8–10 mm wide, that carries the pods. Fix: extrude one shell from the LCD outline + 9 mm, with a 50° outer wall. Delete the rod. Seat the pod cradle on it.
3. **The gaps are not black, and the finish is too clean.** The creases under the S-step and round the battery block measure 25–40, not below 12. Scratches are almost invisible. The right-end barrel (x 1380–1560, y 660–860) is plain plastic, where the reference has speckled gunmetal. Fix: gap channels 3 mm deep or more, over an albedo-0.01 liner. Add 20–40 straight hairline scratches per plate (+30 value). Give the barrel the gunmetal.

5. **Research check** — The fillets and screws agree. Three findings are contradicted. The steps are beads, not broad sloped faces. The grain is lumpy normal noise, not a fine speckle. The slopes are not lighter than the tops. The cord plug (x 1380–1440, y 520–620) stands like a post; the reference has a dome lying on the body.

6. **What 8.5 needs**
- Steps 6–8 mm high on flat 45–55° faces. No polymer bump.
- A solid LCD shield under the pods. No rod.
- Creases below 12, <12 % at 5.3 or more, backdrop TL at 108 or less.
- Fine scratches, albedo speckle, a gunmetal barrel.
- A black gear with lit crests. The arm runs down the flank to the hex lug.
