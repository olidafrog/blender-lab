# Calibration: A vs B

The two renders are the same scene. A pixel diff puts all the changes in two places: the LCD shield and the sloped faces of the S-step and the lower battery step. Everything else matches pixel for pixel.

Problems both renders share: the body is a squat, wide slab. The reference is a taller shell with a clear upper-left notch. The twin pods lie flat instead of standing up by the antenna. The dial is oversized bright chrome. The right-side thumb-wheel stack is mostly hidden. The polymer has uniform grain but no edge-wear speckle, and only a few scratches. The metal reads as CG chrome. The flat tops are about 70–80 (hit), and the sloped steps read lighter (hit).
