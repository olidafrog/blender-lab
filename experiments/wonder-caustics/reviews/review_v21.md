## Score: 8.4 / 10 — not accepted (threshold 8.5)

Four of round 6's six notes are genuinely closed. The mid-tones have come down: non-band areas now
fall to near-black instead of steel grey, and the frame reads as glass on black rather than brushed
metal, which is the single biggest gain. The peanut has its ribbon back through the waist and rejoins
the family. The square crop is right — the mark fills the frame and the dead lower-right block is
gone. And a focal form has finally appeared by design: bar 2's blue → white → orange sweep leads, with
the warm lower bar supporting it, which is the warm/cool split `ref_ring` runs.

What still separates it from the references is the ramp itself, the note I called the whole remaining
gap. A band here is warm on one side of a blown white core and cool on the other — a two-ended
dispersion fringe, not a spectrum. In `ref_puck` red, yellow, green, cyan and blue sit adjacent at
comparable width inside one band; here green and magenta appear only as one- and two-pixel edge
fringes, so the sweep is really a three-stop. Against the softer `ref_ring` v21 is close; against
`ref_puck` and `ref_abstract` it is still noticeably narrower in hue and lower in saturation. Two
concrete defects also cost it: a speckled, dashed RGB comb runs along most bevels — bar 2's lower
right, bar 4's base, the peanut's left edge — which reads as chromatic-aberration artefact rather than
refraction; and the peanut carries no warm end at all, so it sits apart from the group as the one
cool-only object.

## Ranked changes

1. **Finish the ramp inside the band — put green and magenta at band width.** The warm-white-cool
   structure is correct but under-resolved. Green exists only as a fringe and magenta not at all, so
   each band is a three-stop where every reference runs six. Increase dispersion (higher Abbe spread)
   or widen the light strip's angular spread further so the sweep resolves its middle rather than
   jumping warm to cool across the highlight. This is still the whole remaining gap.
2. **Kill the speckled bevel comb.** The dashed multicolour dotting along the bevels — clearest on
   bar 2's lower right, bar 4's lower edge and the peanut's left rim — reads as a sampling or
   dispersion-sample artefact, not as glass. The references have clean continuous fringes on every
   edge. Raise dispersion samples, soften the bevel, or reduce the number of spectral samples per
   strip so the fringe resolves as a smooth line.
3. **Give the peanut a warm end.** It is now cyan → blue → green with a single red fleck at the crown.
   Every other form carries both halves of the spectrum; the peanut reads as a different material
   because it does not. One orange or red pass through the lower lobe would seat it in the group.
4. **Narrow the blown white cores.** Bars 2, 3 and 4 each have a wide flat blown highlight down their
   length, and it is eating the part of the band where green and yellow should live. `ref_puck` blows
   to white only in a thin line at the ramp's peak. Drop light intensity slightly or narrow the strip
   so the core is a line, not a column.
5. **Lift overall saturation a step.** With the greys now gone the hues can take more without tipping
   to neon. The oranges on bars 3 and 5 are closer to tan than to the references' clean chroma, and
   the cool half sits at a teal-grey rather than a true cyan.
6. **Break the vertical parallelism at the top.** Bars 1 and 2 both run their band down the same
   frame-vertical axis at nearly the same pitch, which is the one place the composition still reads
   mechanical. Vary one of the two light angles by 10–15°.

## Keep

- **The recovered blacks.** Non-band areas falling to true black instead of 30–40% grey is the round's
  biggest win and the thing that made the material read as perspex. Do not trade it back for coverage.
- **The square crop.** The mark filling the frame is correct — hold it.
- **The focal hierarchy.** Bar 2 leading with the warm lower bar supporting closes a note open since
  round 3. Do not flatten it by pushing every form to equal intensity.
- **The peanut's restored ribbon.** Right structurally; it only needs the warm half.
- **The warm-versus-cool split per form.** This is `ref_ring`'s core move and v21 has it.
- **The 18° rotation and the ribbons bending through the elbows.** Intact from v20.
- The pure black world, no floor, no spill. Correct across all seven rounds.
- The silhouette, the legibility of the forms, and the restrained surface micro-detail.
