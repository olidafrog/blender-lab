# Art direction review — v18

Reviewed 2026-09-19 against `ref_ring.png`, `ref_puck.png`, `ref_abstract.png`, carried forward from
`review_v06b.md` (5.6), `review_v11a.md` (6.8), `review_v13b.md` (7.4) and `review_v16.md` (7.9).
Candidate: `renders/v18.png` (40% scale, 160 samples). `renders/v16.png` and `renders/v17a.png` seen
for calibration only, not scored. Noise and resolution ignored.

## Score: 8.3 / 10 — not accepted (threshold 8.5)

The biggest single-round gain so far, and the two faults that held v16 down are genuinely fixed. The
concentric islands are gone: colour now runs as ribbons down the length of each bar, which is the
behaviour `ref_abstract` shows. The ramp is complete for the first time — magenta, red, orange,
yellow, green, cyan and blue all appear at real width, green included, and the rust/tobacco cast has
cleared to clean spectral hues. Value range is intact: blown white cores against true black, deep
saturation without tipping to neon except in one place. The peanut has gone from the weakest form to
one of the strongest.

What keeps it under the bar is band frequency and band direction. The references carry *few, wide*
bands with large calm areas between them — `ref_puck` has one dominant ramp crossing a near-black
field; `ref_ring` has one orange sweep and one blue sweep per surface. v18 has perhaps fifteen thin
stripes per bar, evenly pitched, and at that frequency the surface starts to read as holographic foil
or a chromatic-aberration comb rather than thick perspex. The left bar is the worst of it: a picket
fence with no rest anywhere in the form. Second, every stripe on all five bars runs parallel to the
frame's vertical, so the colour ignores the geometry — the bands drive straight through the elbows
and bevels instead of bending where the form turns. In both references the spectrum is *owned by the
curvature*; here it is overlaid on it. Third, the middle bar's yellow-orange-magenta stack is the one
place saturation goes poster-flat, and the dark chrome caps on the bar tops still carry no hue.

## Ranked changes

1. **Cut the band count by roughly half to two thirds, and widen what remains.** Target four to six
   ribbons per bar, not fifteen. Widen the light strips, reduce the number of strips, or flatten the
   crown's cross-section so the refraction angle sweeps more slowly across the bar width. Some of each
   bar should be calm — near-black or clean white — as it is in `ref_puck`. This is the single change
   that separates "prismatic glass" from "holographic sticker" and it is worth the whole remaining
   gap.
2. **Let the ribbons follow the geometry through the turns.** Right now every band is frame-vertical,
   so the elbow of each zig-zag is crossed by straight stripes. Rotate each bar's crown axis to its
   own local length rather than to world Z, or bend the crown through the corner, so the colour turns
   with the form. `ref_ring` gets most of its quality from exactly this.
3. **Break the strict parallelism between bars.** All five carry the same pitch and the same vertical
   direction, which reads mechanical. Vary the light angle by 10–20° per form, or vary crown depth so
   one bar runs wide slow ribbons and another runs tighter ones. This also delivers the focal point
   asked for in rounds 3 and 4 — still outstanding.
4. **Pull the magenta down on the middle bar.** The yellow → orange → hot magenta stack is the one
   patch reading as printed ink rather than refracted light. Drop that band's saturation a little or
   let it blow toward white at its core, as the neighbouring ramps do.
5. **Put hue into the top caps.** The bar tops and the upper right bevels are still flat dark
   grey-chrome. Angle them a few degrees into a light band so every visible face does spectral work.
6. **Tighten the right and lower-right of the frame.** The crop is no longer clipping, which is
   correct, but the lower-right quadrant is now a large empty block and the gap between the peanut and
   the bars is the widest dead area in the image. The peanut cannot move, so close the frame around
   the group instead: crop in from the right and bottom until the mark sits at about 88–90% of frame
   height.

## Keep

- **The ribbon structure.** This is the round's win. Colour runs the length of the form instead of
  ringing into islands. Do not retreat toward v16's concentric pattern when reducing band count.
- **The completed spectrum.** Green is real and full width, magenta and blue are present, and the
  warm end is clean orange-red instead of rust. Round 4's two colour notes are closed.
- **The neutral volume absorption.** Removing the brown/amber tint is what let the hues come clean.
  v17a shows the cost of getting this wrong — that olive cast killed the whole frame.
- **The value range.** Blown cores next to true black, carried forward intact from v16.
- The pure black world, no floor, no spill. Correct across all five rounds.
- The silhouette, and the restrained level of surface micro-detail.
- The peanut's treatment specifically — it now reads as the same material as the bars.
