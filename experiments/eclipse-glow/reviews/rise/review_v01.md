# Review v01 — rise video

1. **Score:** 6.7 / 10

2. **What works**
- The rested logo (f204–288, crop f270) keeps the still's look: navy-to-pink body, spectral rim, crescents, grain. It is fully legible.
- The mirror is correct. It flips, squashes to about 0.6, breaks up at the edges and fades with depth (f160 crop: the step and the dot blob read upside-down).
- The caps come up first as flat, squashed crescents (f60). This is the best moonrise beat in the piece.

3. **Problems, ranked**
1. **The piece is dead for about half its length.** In f1 and f24 there is only the horizon line. The rise runs from about f40 to f190. Then f204, f228 and f288 are the same frame: the logo is parked and nothing moves for about 4 s. The rise itself then feels hurried. Fix: start the caps at f12–18 and ease the rise out over f18–240 (a long ease-out, with the last 10 % of travel taking about 2 s). Keep a slow drift of 4–8 px up to f288. Keep the haze and mirror moving to the last frame.
2. **The shimmer does not boil, and it stops at the horizon.** f160 and f161 are almost identical: the ripple on the bar edges and in the reflection has the same phase. The layering above the horizon only reaches about 40 px (f160). In f96 and f132 the rising body has clean edges. Fix: raise the time phase so the offset changes 30–60 % of a wavelength per frame. Extend the above-horizon haze to 150–250 px (at 1080p), with a falloff. Use 3–6 px horizontal offset in bands 4–10 px tall at the line, going to 0 at the top of the band.
3. **Extinction is too weak on the rising logo, and it lingers when risen.** In f60 and f96 the caps and rims are at full rest brightness and colour, so the logo is not dimmer when low. At rest (f270) the bar bottoms are still salmon/coral, not the still's lilac-pink. Fix: key the dim-and-redden to screen height, not to logo height. Within 100 px of the horizon, drop value by 50–65 % and shift the hue toward orange. Fade this to 0 by 250 px, so the rested logo matches the still.

Minor: the horizon is a thin neon laser line at full brightness across the whole frame, even in f1. It reads as synthwave rather than atmosphere. Halve the core line and let the haze band carry it. The halo at rest is tighter than in the still. Widen the glow radius by about 1.3×.

4. **Research check**
- The mirror (4) and flattening (2) agree with the research.
- Extinction (1) is contradicted: the logo is not dimmer when low, and it stays redder when high.
- Heat haze (3) is only partly met. It is confined to the reflection and a thin band, and it is nearly frozen from frame to frame.
- All four effects should fade with height. Only the mirror does.

5. **What 8.5 needs**
- Retime: no empty opening, a long eased rise to about f240, and a slow drift after.
- Shimmer that visibly changes each frame and reaches 150–250 px above the line.
- Strong extinction low down that clears fully at rest, so the rested frame matches the still.
- A softer horizon line.
